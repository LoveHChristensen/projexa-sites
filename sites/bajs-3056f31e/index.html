01 / What we believe

Life isn’t asking
to be optimized.

It’s asking to be noticed. Bajs began with a simple question: what if our everyday systems made us feel more like ourselves, not less?

We make gentle tools, prompts and rhythms for the parts of life that are already happening — the morning kitchen, the open notebook, the walk home.
